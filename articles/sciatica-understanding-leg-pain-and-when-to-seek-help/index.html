Sciatica: Understanding Leg Pain and When to Seek Help

Learn about sciatica, its causes, and when to see a doctor.

Shivwik Holistic Care team · November 2026 · 2 min read
Home › Articles › Sciatica: Understanding Leg Pain and When to Seek Help

What is Sciatica?

Sciatica refers to pain that starts in the lower back and travels down one leg. This pain can vary from a dull ache to a sharp or burning sensation. Understanding the causes, symptoms, and when to seek help can be crucial for effective management.

Symptoms of Sciatica

  • Pain in the lower back or buttock that extends down the leg
  • Weakness, tingling, or numbness in the leg or foot
  • Pain that worsens with coughing or sneezing
  • Pain that improves when you lie down

Causes of Sciatica

Sciatica is often caused by a herniated or bulging disc, bone spurs, or narrowing of the spine (spinal stenosis). It can also result from muscle strain or poor posture.

When to Seek Help

  • If your pain is severe or sudden
  • If you have numbness or weakness in your leg
  • If you experience loss of bladder or bowel control
  • If your pain does not improve or worsens over time

Gentle Exercises for Sciatica

Before starting any exercise routine, consult with a physiotherapist at Shivwik Holistic Care. Gently try:

  • Pelvic tilts: Lie on your back, place your hands under your hips, and slowly lift your pelvis off the ground, hold for a few seconds, then lower back down.
  • Hamstring stretches: Sit on the floor with one leg straight and the other bent. Reach towards your toes on the straight leg, hold for 15 seconds, then switch legs.
  • Glute squeezes: Sit on the edge of a chair, squeeze your buttocks together, and hold for 5 seconds. Repeat 10 times.

Warning Signs

Seek immediate medical help if you experience:

  • Loss of bladder or bowel control
  • Severe pain that does not improve with over-the-counter pain relievers
  • Weakness or numbness in your leg or foot
  • Fever, chills, or weight loss
