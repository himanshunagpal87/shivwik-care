Ankle Sprains: Why Proper Rehabilitation is Key

Learn how rehabilitation helps prevent future sprains and keeps you active.

Shivwik Holistic Care team · November 2026 · 2 min read
Home › Articles › Ankle Sprains: Why Proper Rehabilitation is Key

Understanding Ankle Sprains

Ankle sprains are common injuries that can happen to anyone, from athletes to everyday people. They usually occur when the ankle twists, causing the ligaments to stretch or tear. While they can be painful, with proper care and rehabilitation, you can recover fully and prevent future sprains.

Why Rehabilitation is Important

  • Strengthening the Ankle: Rehabilitation exercises help strengthen the muscles around the ankle, providing better support and stability.
  • Improving Flexibility: Flexibility exercises keep the ankle flexible, which reduces the risk of future sprains.
  • Enhancing Coordination: Coordination exercises improve how the ankle works with other parts of the foot, reducing the risk of injury.
  • Avoiding Weakness: Weakness in the ankle increases the likelihood of re-injury. Rehabilitation ensures the ankle remains strong.

Gentle Rehabilitation Exercises

Before starting any exercises, get an assessment from a physiotherapist. Stop if you feel increased pain.

  • Ankle Rolls: Place a towel under your foot and roll it from side to side. Do 10-15 repetitions.
  • Foot Alphabet: Draw the alphabet with your foot on the floor. This helps improve ankle movement and coordination.
  • Calf Raises: Stand next to a wall for support. Raise and lower your heels. Do 10-15 repetitions.

Warning Signs

Seek medical advice if you experience:

  • Increased pain
  • Swelling that doesn’t go down
  • A popping sound when moving your ankle
  • Inability to put weight on your foot
