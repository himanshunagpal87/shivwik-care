Joint Stiffness in Winter: Staying Mobile with Gentle Exercises

Learn how to keep your joints flexible during cold weather with simple exercises and tips to avoid stiffness.

Shivwik Holistic Care team · October 2026 · 2 min read
Home › Articles › Joint Stiffness in Winter: Staying Mobile with Gentle Exercises

Coping with Cold Weather

As the temperatures drop, many experience joint stiffness, especially in the mornings. This common issue can be managed with the right approach. At Shivwik Holistic Care, we offer guidance to help you stay mobile and reduce discomfort.

Simple Exercises for Joint Stiffness

Gentle Range of Motion Exercises

  • Shoulders: Gently rotate your shoulders in both directions. Hold each rotation for 5 seconds.
  • Hips: Sit on a chair and gently swing your legs from side to side. Repeat this 10 times.
  • Fingers and Ankles: Flex and extend your fingers and ankles. Do this for 10 repetitions.

Warm-Up Routine

Before performing any exercises, warm up with light activity such as walking or gentle stretching. This helps to increase blood flow and prepare your muscles and joints for movement.

Daily Routine

Incorporate these exercises into your daily routine. Perform them in the morning to help ease stiffness and throughout the day to maintain flexibility.

Warning Signs

If you experience sudden swelling, severe pain, or if your joints become difficult to move, contact your doctor promptly. These could be signs of an underlying condition that requires medical attention.

Consultation at Shivwik Holistic Care

If you’re struggling with joint stiffness, consider booking an assessment at Shivwik Holistic Care. Our physiotherapists can provide personalized advice and exercises to help you manage your symptoms. Remember, if your condition worsens, seek medical advice.

By following these simple tips, you can keep your joints moving and reduce stiffness during the colder months.
