Recovering from a Stroke with Physiotherapy

Learn how physiotherapy helps stroke survivors regain mobility and independence.

Shivwik Holistic Care team · September 2026 · 2 min read
Home › Articles › Recovering from a Stroke with Physiotherapy

Understanding Stroke Rehabilitation

A stroke can be life-changing, affecting how you move, speak, and think. However, with the right support, you can regain much of your independence. Physiotherapy is a key part of the recovery journey, helping you rebuild your strength and mobility step by step.

What Does Physiotherapy Involve?

Physiotherapists use a variety of techniques tailored to your needs. This may include:

  • Gentle Exercises: These help improve your range of motion and strength. Always get a thorough assessment before starting any exercises.
  • Strengthening Exercises: Building muscle strength is crucial for regaining mobility.
  • Balance and Coordination Drills: These exercises help you regain stability and reduce the risk of falls.
  • Functional Training: Practicing everyday activities to help you relearn skills and improve daily living.

The Role of Gentle Exercises

Gentle exercises are key to recovery. Here are a few examples:

  • Arm Exercises: Gentle arm lifts and rotations can help improve circulation and strength.
  • Leg Exercises: Gently stretching and strengthening your legs can improve circulation and reduce the risk of blood clots.
  • Breathing Exercises: These help improve lung function, which can be compromised after a stroke.

Always stop if you experience any pain or discomfort.

Warning Signs

Some warning signs need prompt medical attention:

  • Sudden dizziness or difficulty walking
  • Persistent headaches
  • Changes in vision or speech
  • New or worsening confusion
