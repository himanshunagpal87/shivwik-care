About Us

Founder-Led, Hospital-Grade Physiotherapy Care

Founder & Lead Physiotherapist

Dr. Sonal Khera Nagpal

BPT, MPT (Cardiopulmonary), COMT, CMP, CPT, CKT, RPYT, Garbhsanskar Coach, DCPT, MIAP, NCABH

Dr. Sonal founded Shivwik Holistic Care to bring personalized, evidence-based physiotherapy to Paschim Vihar — care shaped by real hospital experience across critical care, orthopedics, neurology and multi-specialty rehabilitation.

Her clinical background spans ICU critical care at St. Stephen's Hospital, orthopedic and neurological rehabilitation at Bhagwan Mahaveer Hospital, and multi-specialty physiotherapy at Bhatia Global Hospital — experience she now brings directly into one-on-one, individualized treatment plans for every patient at Shivwik.

Beyond general physiotherapy, she is also a certified Garbhsanskar coach, bringing specialized antenatal and holistic wellness guidance to expecting mothers.
