Privacy & Terms

Your Information & Using This Website

Plain-language answers to what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Who we are

Shivwik Holistic Care is a physiotherapy and rehabilitation clinic at 182, Ambica Vihar, Paschim Vihar, New Delhi 110087. For anything on this page, contact us at info@shivwikholisticcare.com or +91 74074 46000.

What we collect

  • Appointment requests from this website: your name, WhatsApp number, the service you need, your preferred time and any message you add. We verify your number with a one-time code sent on WhatsApp.
  • Questions sent through our contact form: your name, email address and message.
  • WhatsApp conversations with our clinic number, including our automated booking assistant.
  • Clinical records created when you visit, such as your history, assessments and treatment notes.

How we use it

To book, confirm and remind you about appointments, provide and follow up on your treatment, answer your questions, and ask for feedback after a visit. We do not sell your information. We share it only with the service providers who help us run the clinic (for example hosting, database, messaging and email services) and when the law requires it.

WhatsApp and our automated assistant

Messages to our clinic number may first be answered by an automated assistant that can share clinic information and book appointments. Our clinic team can see these conversations. If you would rather speak to a person, just say so in the chat or call reception.

Patient ratings on this website

After a visit we may ask you on WhatsApp to rate your experience from 1 to 5. That message tells you that ratings of 4 or 5 may be shown on this website.

  • Your agreement: if you reply with a 4 or 5 after seeing that notice, you agree to your rating being shown on this website as described here.
  • What is shown: only the star rating, your initials (for example “R.S.”) and the month. Never your full name, phone number, condition or treatment.
  • The overall score (for example “4.7 from 52 patient ratings”) is calculated from all ratings, including lower ones, and does not identify anyone.
  • Ratings of 3 or below are never shown individually. We use them to improve, and our team may contact you to understand what went wrong.
  • Changing your mind: reply STOP to our WhatsApp number at any time, tell our reception, or email us. We will remove your rating from the website promptly. This never affects your care.

Google reviews

Reviews you post on Google are public and governed by Google's own terms. We may link to them, or show them on this website with attribution to Google.

Health articles on this website

Our articles, news and event pages are general information to help you understand common conditions and how physiotherapy may help. They are not medical advice, a diagnosis or a substitute for an assessment by a qualified professional, and reading them does not create a patient relationship with us. If you have severe or sudden symptoms, please see a doctor promptly.

Booking through this website

A request sent from this website is not a confirmed appointment until our clinic confirms it with you on WhatsApp. Please give accurate details, and let us know as early as possible if you need to cancel or reschedule.

Keeping your information safe

Your information is stored with access controls, so staff can see only what their role needs. We keep clinical records for as long as the law requires, and other information only as long as we need it for the purposes above.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, withdraw consent you have given, and raise a grievance. Contact us at info@shivwikholisticcare.com and we will respond as soon as we can.

Changes to this page

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
