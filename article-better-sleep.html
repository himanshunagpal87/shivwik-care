Are You Sleeping Properly?

How physiotherapy can help your body relax, recover and rest better.

Shivwik Holistic Care team · September 2026 · 4 min read

Good sleep is when the body repairs and recharges. But many people find it hard to fall asleep or stay asleep because their body simply can't settle — tight muscles, a stiff neck or back, an uncomfortable posture, or a mind that won't switch off. When these physical factors are part of the problem, physiotherapy can help.

How physiotherapy supports better sleep

  • Relieves muscle tension. Hands-on techniques and targeted exercises help release tight muscles that can make it hard to get comfortable at night.
  • Improves flexibility. Gentle stretching and mobility work reduce stiffness, so turning over or finding a comfortable position is easier.
  • Calms the nervous system. Relaxation and breathing techniques can help the body move out of a “tense” state and prepare for rest.
  • Corrects posture & alignment. Poor posture during the day can leave the neck, shoulders and back aching by bedtime. Posture correction and strengthening address this at the source.
  • Personalised treatment. Every person's body and routine is different. Your physiotherapist assesses what is affecting you and designs a plan around your needs.

When to consider seeing a physiotherapist

If aches, stiffness or tension in your neck, shoulders, back or joints are keeping you up at night — or you wake up feeling sore rather than rested — an assessment can help identify the physical causes and what can be done about them.
