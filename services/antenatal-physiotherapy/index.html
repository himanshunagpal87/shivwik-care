Antenatal physiotherapy and Garbhsanskar guidance

Home › Services › Antenatal Physiotherapy and Garbhsanskar

Antenatal physiotherapy helps with the aches, posture changes and breathing demands of pregnancy through safe exercise, positioning and advice. At Shivwik Holistic Care in Paschim Vihar it is offered by Dr. Sonal Khera Nagpal, who is also a certified Garbhsanskar coach, and it follows your obstetrician's advice.

What can antenatal physiotherapy help with?

  • lower back and pelvic girdle pain
  • neck, shoulder and rib discomfort as posture changes
  • leg cramps and swelling
  • pelvic floor awareness and exercises
  • breathing and relaxation techniques
  • staying active safely through each trimester

What happens in a session?

We ask about your pregnancy, any advice or restrictions from your obstetrician, and what is troubling you. The session may include gentle exercises, supported positions, hands-on techniques that are suitable in pregnancy, and guidance on sitting, sleeping, lifting and getting out of bed comfortably.

What is Garbhsanskar guidance?

Garbhsanskar is a traditional Indian approach to wellbeing in pregnancy that includes relaxation, positive routines, gentle movement and mindful practices. At our clinic it is offered as wellness guidance alongside physiotherapy. It is not a medical treatment and does not replace antenatal care from your obstetrician.

Is exercise safe during pregnancy?

For most women with an uncomplicated pregnancy, regular gentle exercise is considered safe and helpful. If your obstetrician has advised rest or has identified a complication, please tell us and bring the advice with you. We plan only within what your doctor allows.

When should I contact my obstetrician?

Contact your obstetrician or go to hospital without delay if you have vaginal bleeding or fluid loss, regular painful contractions, reduced movements of the baby, severe headache, blurred vision, chest pain, calf pain or swelling, or dizziness and fainting.

Questions patients ask

From which month can I start antenatal physiotherapy?

You can come at any stage if your pregnancy is progressing normally and your obstetrician has no objection. The exercises are chosen for your trimester.

Do you offer physiotherapy after delivery?

Please ask us on WhatsApp or by phone and we will guide you based on the type of delivery and your doctor's advice.

Can my family attend the Garbhsanskar sessions?

Please ask when booking and we will let you know what is suitable.
