Rehabilitation after surgery, fracture or injury

Home › Services › Rehabilitation After Surgery, Fracture or Injury

Rehabilitation after surgery, a fracture or a major injury restores movement, strength and confidence in stages. At Shivwik Holistic Care in Paschim Vihar the plan follows your surgeon's or doctor's instructions and progresses as the tissue heals and your ability returns.

Who is this for?

  • after a joint replacement of the knee or hip
  • after ligament, tendon or other orthopaedic surgery
  • after a fracture, once your doctor allows movement
  • after a long period in a cast, brace or bed

Why does the surgeon's advice matter?

After an operation or fracture, there are limits on how much weight and movement the area can take, and these change week by week. Please bring your discharge summary and any written protocol from your surgeon. We work within those limits and can speak with your doctor when needed.

What does rehabilitation include?

  • Swelling and pain management in the early phase
  • Regaining range of movement without stressing the repair
  • Strengthening, increased gradually
  • Walking practice, including safe use of a walker, crutches or stick, and stairs
  • Balance and daily tasks: getting up from a chair, in and out of bed, the bathroom and the car

Dr. Sonal's clinical background includes orthopaedic and neurological rehabilitation at Bhagwan Mahaveer Hospital.

What can I do at home?

You will get a short home programme at each stage. Doing it regularly matters more than doing a lot at once. Stop and tell us if an exercise causes sharp pain, new swelling or a feeling that the joint is giving way.

When should I contact my doctor?

Contact your surgeon or go to a hospital if you have a hot, red, increasingly swollen wound, discharge from the wound, fever, calf pain or swelling, sudden breathlessness, or a fall onto the operated limb.

Questions patients ask

When should physiotherapy start after surgery?

Your surgeon decides. For many operations it begins in hospital and continues after discharge. Bring your discharge papers and we will plan from the stage you are at.

How long will recovery take?

It differs with the operation, your health before it and how you respond. We review your progress regularly and explain what each stage is aiming for.

My cast came off and the joint is very stiff. Is that normal?

Stiffness and weakness are common after immobilisation and usually improve with guided movement. Get it assessed so that the exercises match what your fracture allows.
