Pulmonary rehabilitation for COPD and asthma

Home › Services › Cardiopulmonary Rehabilitation in Paschim Vihar, Delhi › Pulmonary Rehabilitation for COPD and Asthma

Pulmonary rehabilitation combines breathing techniques, airway clearance and graded exercise to help people with COPD, asthma and other long-term lung conditions manage breathlessness and stay active. At Shivwik Holistic Care in Paschim Vihar it is led by a physiotherapist trained in cardiopulmonary care and works alongside your chest physician's treatment.

How can physiotherapy help with COPD or asthma?

Medicines and inhalers prescribed by your doctor remain the main treatment. Physiotherapy adds skills and fitness that may make daily life easier:

  • Breathing control, such as pursed-lip and relaxed breathing, to manage breathlessness
  • Airway-clearance techniques when phlegm is hard to bring up
  • Graded walking and strengthening, because staying active helps you do more with the breath you have
  • Pacing and energy saving for stairs, bathing, housework and travel
  • Posture and chest mobility work

What happens at the first visit?

We ask about your symptoms, inhalers, flare-ups and what you find difficult. We check your breathing pattern and how you respond to gentle activity. Please bring your prescriptions and any lung function or chest reports.

Delhi air and breathing

On days when the air quality is poor, it is sensible to exercise indoors and follow your doctor's advice about masks and medicines. We can show you a simple indoor routine so that a bad-air day does not stop your programme.

When should I see a doctor urgently?

Seek medical help straight away if you are too breathless to speak in full sentences, your reliever inhaler is not helping, your lips look bluish, or you have chest pain or a high fever. Physiotherapy is not a substitute for emergency care.

Questions patients ask

Can breathing exercises replace my inhaler?

No. Breathing exercises are used alongside the medicines your doctor has prescribed. Please do not stop or reduce an inhaler without your doctor's advice.

I get breathless when I walk. Should I avoid exercise?

Avoiding activity often makes breathlessness worse over time. Supervised, graded exercise is a standard part of pulmonary rehabilitation, and we set the level after assessing you.

Is pulmonary rehabilitation useful for older people?

Age alone is not a barrier. The programme is set to your ability and adjusted as you progress.
