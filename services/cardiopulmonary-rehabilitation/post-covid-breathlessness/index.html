Post-COVID physiotherapy for breathlessness and fatigue

Home › Services › Cardiopulmonary Rehabilitation in Paschim Vihar, Delhi › Post-COVID Physiotherapy for Breathlessness

Some people stay breathless, tired or weak for weeks or months after COVID or another chest infection. Physiotherapy may help through breathing exercises, carefully paced activity and gradual strengthening. At Shivwik Holistic Care in Paschim Vihar we assess you first and set the pace to how your body responds.

What problems do people report after COVID?

Common ones are breathlessness on small effort, tiredness that does not match the activity, a lingering cough, chest tightness, poor sleep and loss of strength after bed rest. These can have several causes, so a medical check comes first.

How does physiotherapy help?

  • Breathing exercises to ease breathlessness and settle a fast, shallow breathing pattern
  • Pacing: learning how much activity your body tolerates today and building from there
  • Gradual strengthening and walking, increased slowly
  • Positions of ease for breathlessness and for sleep
  • Airway clearance if phlegm remains

Why is pacing so important?

After COVID, some people feel much worse a day or two after doing too much. If that happens to you, pushing harder is not the answer. We keep activity within what you tolerate and increase it only when your body allows.

When should I see a doctor first?

Please see a doctor before starting if you have chest pain, palpitations, fainting, breathlessness at rest, swelling in a leg, or symptoms that are getting worse instead of better. Bring any reports and prescriptions to your first visit.

Questions patients ask

I had COVID months ago and still get breathless. Is it too late for physiotherapy?

It is reasonable to get assessed at any stage. Please also let your doctor know that the breathlessness has continued.

Will I be made to exercise hard?

No. The programme starts at a level you can manage and progresses gradually, guided by how you feel during and after each session.

Can I do the breathing exercises at home?

Yes. We teach you a short routine to practise at home and review it at your visits.
