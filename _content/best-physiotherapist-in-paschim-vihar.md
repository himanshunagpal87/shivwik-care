## What makes a physiotherapist the best choice for you?

"Best" depends on what you need. These are the things worth checking before you book with any physiotherapist or physiotherapy centre:

- **Qualification**: a recognised degree in physiotherapy (BPT), and ideally a master's degree (MPT) in the area you need help with.
- **Experience with your condition**: a heart or lung problem, a joint replacement and a sports injury each need different skills.
- **An assessment first**: a good physiotherapist examines you and reads your reports before starting treatment.
- **Time with the physiotherapist**: you should be treated by the qualified person, not left alone on a machine.
- **A clear plan**: what the problem is, what the treatment involves, what you should do at home and how progress will be checked.
- **Honesty**: no promise of a cure, and a referral to a doctor when something is outside physiotherapy.
- **Practical things**: location, timings, easy booking and clear communication.
