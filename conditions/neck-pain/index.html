Physiotherapy for neck pain and cervical pain

Home › Neck Pain and Cervical Pain Physiotherapy

Neck pain and stiffness often come from posture, long screen time and muscle tension, and usually respond to movement, exercise and better daily habits. Physiotherapy at Shivwik Holistic Care in Paschim Vihar includes an assessment, manual therapy for stiff joints and tight muscles, and exercises you can continue at home.

What causes neck pain?

Common contributors are long hours looking down at a phone or laptop, a poorly set-up desk, sleeping in an awkward position, stress and age-related changes in the neck, often called cervical spondylosis. An assessment looks at which of these apply to you. This page does not diagnose your pain.

How does physiotherapy help?

  • Manual therapy to ease stiff joints and tight muscles in the neck and upper back
  • Exercises to restore movement and strengthen the neck and shoulder-blade muscles
  • Posture and desk advice for work, phone use and driving
  • Pillow and sleep position guidance

What can I do myself?

  • Raise your screen to eye level and bring the phone up instead of bending your head down.
  • Change position every half hour.
  • Keep the neck moving gently within comfort.
  • Use heat for muscle tightness if it feels soothing.

When should I see a doctor first?

See a doctor promptly if neck pain follows an accident or fall, or comes with weakness, numbness or tingling in the arms or legs, loss of balance, severe headache, fever, difficulty swallowing, or problems with bladder or bowel control.

Questions patients ask

What is cervical pain?

"Cervical" refers to the neck part of the spine. Cervical pain is pain felt in the neck, sometimes spreading to the shoulder, upper back or arm.

Should I wear a neck collar?

A collar is not usually needed for ordinary neck pain, and long use can weaken the neck muscles. Use one only if a doctor or physiotherapist has advised it for your condition.

Can neck problems cause arm pain or tingling?

They can, when a nerve in the neck is irritated. Arm symptoms should be assessed, and weakness or spreading numbness needs a doctor's opinion.
